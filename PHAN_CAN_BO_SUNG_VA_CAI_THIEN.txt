CAC PHAN CAN BO SUNG VA CAI THIEN CHO DO AN MO DUN KANBAN
================================================================

I. TONG QUAN
------------

Mo dun Kanban da hoan thanh phan lon cac chuc nang cot loi: tao bang
Kanban, tao va quan ly card, keo tha card, phan cong thanh vien, deadline,
tep dinh kem, binh luan, lich su thay doi, phan quyen theo group,
backup/restore va mot so kiem thu PHPUnit/Behat.

Tuy nhien, de phu hop day du voi de cuong do an, can bo sung hoac hoan
thien cac noi dung duoi day.


II. CAC CHUC NANG CON THIEU
---------------------------

1. Quan ly cot Kanban

- Cho phep giao vien tao cot moi.
- Cho phep giao vien sua ten cot, mau sac va mo ta cot.
- Cho phep giao vien xoa cot.
- Cho phep thay doi thu tu cac cot.
- Cho phep cau hinh WIP limit cho tung cot.
- Xu ly cac card dang nam trong cot khi cot bi xoa.
- Them external API rieng cho cac thao tac tao, sua, xoa va sap xep cot.

Ly do can bo sung:
Hien tai he thong chu yeu tao ba cot mac dinh To Do, In Progress va Done.
De cuong co noi dung thiet ke bang Kanban co kha nang cau hinh, vi vay can
co giao dien quan ly cot thay vi chi su dung cot mac dinh.


2. Sap xep vi tri card

- Ho tro day du truong newposition.
- Luu thu tu card khi keo card trong cung mot cot.
- Luu thu tu card khi di chuyen card sang cot khac.
- Hien thi dung thu tu card sau khi tai lai trang.
- Dam bao thu tu duoc cap nhat dung khi co nhieu nguoi cung thao tac.

Ly do can bo sung:
Chuc nang keo tha hien da hoat dong, nhung viec luu thu tu card trong cung
mot cot chua duoc ho tro day du. Neu khong luu thu tu, vi tri card co the
bi thay doi sau khi tai lai bang.


3. Thong bao va canh bao deadline

- Gui thong bao Moodle khi card duoc giao cho thanh vien.
- Gui thong bao khi assignee bi thay doi.
- Gui thong bao khi card duoc di chuyen sang cot moi.
- Gui thong bao khi co binh luan moi.
- Gui thong bao truoc khi card den han.
- Gui thong bao khi card bi qua han.
- Tao scheduled task de tu dong kiem tra deadline.
- Cho phep nguoi dung theo doi hoac tat mot so loai thong bao neu can.

Ly do can bo sung:
Hien tai giao dien da hien thi card sap den han va qua han, nhung chua co
co che gui thong bao tu dong. De cuong co noi dung tich hop thong bao va
canh bao tre han, do do day la phan can uu tien cao.


4. Dashboard nang cao

- Them bieu do so luong card theo tung cot.
- Them bieu do tien do theo thoi gian.
- Loc thong ke theo group.
- Loc theo thanh vien.
- Loc theo trang thai card.
- Loc theo khoang deadline.
- Hien thi so card da tao, da cap nhat va da hoan thanh cua tung thanh vien.
- Hien thi ty le hoan thanh cua tung nhom.
- Hien thi danh sach card qua han va sap den han.
- Co the xuat bao cao neu pham vi do an cho phep.

Ly do can bo sung:
Dashboard hien tai moi cung cap thong ke co ban. De phan anh ro muc do
dong gop cua thanh vien va tien do nhom, can mo rong bo loc va bieu do.


5. Tich hop truc tiep voi Moodle Assignment

Can xac dinh ro pham vi cua chuc nang nay. Neu de tai yeu cau tich hop voi
hoat dong Assignment cua Moodle thi can bo sung:

- Cho phep chon mot Assignment co san trong khoa hoc.
- Lien ket card voi Assignment hoac submission cu the.
- Hien thi trang thai da nop/chua nop bai.
- Hien thi diem va feedback neu nguoi dung co quyen xem.
- Theo doi thanh vien da nop bai.
- Tu dong cap nhat trang thai card khi co submission.

Neu khong trien khai tich hop truc tiep, tai lieu can ghi ro rang he thong
chi ho tro luu URL nop bai trong card, khong doc truc tiep du lieu tu module
Assignment.


III. CAC PHAN CAN HOAN THIEN
----------------------------

6. Lich su thao tac day du

Can dam bao history ghi nhan day du cac thao tac:

- Tao card.
- Sua tieu de va mo ta.
- Sua deadline.
- Sua assignee.
- Di chuyen card.
- Xoa card.
- Them va xoa file dinh kem.
- Them binh luan.
- Thay doi cot hoac WIP limit.

Lich su nen hien thi nguoi thao tac, thoi gian, noi dung thay doi truoc va
sau khi cap nhat neu phu hop.


7. Phan quyen va bao mat

- Kiem tra lai quyen cua giao vien, sinh vien, manager va admin.
- Kiem tra sinh vien khong xem duoc card cua group khac.
- Kiem tra khong the truy cap card thuoc Kanban khac bang cach sua ID tren URL
  hoac request AJAX.
- Kiem tra sesskey, context, cmid va capability tren tat ca endpoint ghi du.
- Kiem tra quyen truy cap file dinh kem.
- Kiem tra du lieu khi nguoi dung khong con la thanh vien cua group.


8. Ngon ngu va giao dien

- Chuyen toan bo chuoi hard-code trong PHP, JavaScript va dashboard vao
  language pack.
- Kiem tra day du tieng Viet va tieng Anh.
- Sua cac canh bao ESLint/coding style trong board.js.
- Kiem tra giao dien tren man hinh nho va thiet bi di dong.
- Kiem tra modal, nut bam, bang card va file dinh kem voi cac Moodle theme
  khac nhau.
- Bo sung thong bao loi ro rang khi request AJAX that bai.


9. Xu ly loi va du lieu bien

- Hien thi loi khi deadline khong hop le.
- Hien thi loi khi WIP limit da day.
- Hien thi loi khi file khong dung dinh dang hoac vuot qua dung luong.
- Hien thi loi khi assignee khong thuoc group hop le.
- Xu ly truong hop card hoac column da bi xoa trong luc nguoi dung dang thao tac.
- Khong de request loi tao ra du lieu khong day du.


IV. KIEM THU CAN BO SUNG
------------------------

10. PHPUnit

Can bo sung test cho:

- Tao, sua va xoa column.
- Sap xep column.
- Sap xep card trong cung column.
- Them, sua va xoa assignee.
- Upload, tai xuong va xoa file.
- Them va doc binh luan.
- Ghi nhan history.
- Notification va scheduled task.
- Bao mat file theo group.
- Xoa activity va kiem tra du lieu lien quan duoc xoa.
- Backup/restore day du tat ca bang va file.


11. Behat va kiem thu giao dien

Can kiem tra cac luong:

- Giao vien tao activity Kanban.
- Sinh vien trong group A khong xem duoc card cua group B.
- Tao card, sua card, xoa card va keo tha card.
- Phan cong mot hoac nhieu thanh vien.
- Them file dinh kem va mo chi tiet file.
- Them binh luan.
- Hien thi card sap den han va qua han.
- Hien thi dashboard.
- Quan ly column.
- Backup va restore activity.


12. Kiem thu tuong thich

- Kiem thu tren MariaDB.
- Kiem thu tren PostgreSQL.
- Kiem thu voi nhieu Moodle theme.
- Kiem tra PHP version va Moodle version muc tieu.
- Kiem tra cai dat moi, nang cap tu phien ban cu va go cai dat.
- Kiem tra tren moi truong Docker neu de cuong yeu cau Docker.


V. CAC NOI DUNG CAN CAP NHAT TRONG BAO CAO
-------------------------------------------

13. Lam ro pham vi san pham

Trong bao cao can tach ro:

- Chuc nang da hoan thanh.
- Chuc nang moi chi hien thi tren giao dien.
- Chuc nang chua co co che tu dong.
- Chuc nang du kien phat trien them.

Vi du:

- Hien thi card qua han: da co.
- Gui email/Moodle notification khi qua han: chua co.
- Luu URL nop bai: da co.
- Tich hop truc tiep voi Moodle Assignment: chua co.
- Dashboard co ban: da co.
- Dashboard bieu do va bo loc nang cao: chua co.


14. Cap nhat tieu chi nghiem thu

Nen bo sung cac tieu chi:

- Giao vien co the quan ly cac column.
- Thu tu card duoc luu dung sau khi keo tha.
- Thanh vien nhan duoc thong bao dung su kien.
- Scheduled task gui canh bao deadline dung thoi diem.
- Dashboard loc duoc theo group va thanh vien.
- Khong lo du lieu giua cac group.
- File dinh kem chi duoc tai boi nguoi co quyen.
- Backup/restore phuc hoi dung card, column, file, comment va history.
- PHPUnit va Behat chay thanh cong.
- Plugin cai dat duoc tren moi truong Moodle muc tieu.


VI. THU TU UU TIEN DE THUC HIEN
-------------------------------

Uu tien 1 - Can lam truoc khi nghiem thu:

1. Bo sung thong bao Moodle va scheduled task deadline.
2. Hoan thien quan ly column.
3. Hoan thien sap xep vi tri card.
4. Bo sung test cho cac chuc nang moi.
5. Chay PHPUnit/Behat va sua loi nghiem trong.

Uu tien 2 - Nen lam de nang chat luong do an:

6. Mo rong dashboard voi bo loc va bieu do.
7. Ghi history day du cho moi thao tac.
8. Chuyen het chuoi hard-code sang language pack.
9. Kiem thu MariaDB va PostgreSQL.
10. Kiem tra responsive va nhieu Moodle theme.

Uu tien 3 - Tuy theo pham vi de tai:

11. Tich hop truc tiep voi Moodle Assignment.
12. Them xuat bao cao.
13. Them CI/Docker tu dong cho build va test.


VII. KET LUAN
-------------

Mo dun hien da dap ung phan cot loi cua mot he thong Kanban trong Moodle.
De dap ung day du de cuong, can tap trung vao bon nhom chinh:

1. Quan ly va tuy chinh column.
2. Thong bao va canh bao deadline tu dong.
3. Dashboard nang cao va thong ke dong gop.
4. Kiem thu, bao mat va tich hop day du voi cac thanh phan Moodle.

Neu chua kip trien khai tat ca chuc nang, can ghi ro trong bao cao day la
phuong an phat trien tiep theo, dong thoi dieu chinh tieu chi nghiem thu
cho dung voi pham vi thuc te cua plugin.
