CAC PHAN CAN BO SUNG VA CAI THIEN CHO DO AN MO DUN KANBAN
================================================================

Trang thai duoc doi chieu voi ma nguon mod/kanban. Cac muc "da co" la
nhan xet tu ma nguon, khong dong nghia voi viec da duoc kiem thu tren moi
truong nghiem thu.

I. TONG QUAN
------------

Plugin da co bang Kanban, card, keo tha, phan cong, deadline, tep, binh luan,
lich su, phan quyen group, dashboard co bieu do, thong bao va backup/restore.
Viec con lai la kiem chung cac luong, sua cac noi dung tai lieu cu va xac
nhan pham vi Assignment truoc khi nghiem thu.


II. CAC CHUC NANG CON THIEU
---------------------------

1. Quan ly cot Kanban - DA CO

- Co API tao, sua, xoa cot rong va sap xep cot; co the sua ten, mau, mo ta
  va WIP limit.
- Cot mac dinh la To Do, In Progress va Done.
- WIP limit duoc kiem tra o server khi tao card qua API/AJAX va endpoint upload,
  va khi chuyen card sang cot khac. Thao tac sap xep trong cung cot khong bi chan.
- Cot co card khong the xoa; can chuyen hoac xoa card truoc.

Da bo sung PHPUnit cho API tao/sua/xoa/sap xep cot va dieu kien khong xoa cot
con card. WIP da co test tao va chuyen card; endpoint upload va thao tac dong
thoi van can kiem thu rieng neu tieu chi nghiem thu yeu cau.


2. Sap xep vi tri card - DA CO, CAN KIEM THU

API nhan newposition va cap nhat sortorder trong cot dich; khi chuyen cot,
sortorder cot cu cung duoc sap xep lai. Da co PHPUnit kiem tra sap xep trong
cung cot, chen dung vi tri khi chuyen cot va danh lai thu tu cot nguon.
Can kiem tra tai lai trang tren giao dien Moodle.


3. Thong bao va canh bao deadline - DA CO MOT PHAN

Plugin co Moodle message providers, thong bao assignee/cap nhat va scheduled
task moi 15 phut de gui mot so canh bao deadline/tien do. Can chay cron va
kiem thu tung tinh huong, chong gui lap, va cai dat bat/tat.

Can xac nhan them: de cuong yeu cau thong bao va canh bao tre han nhung khong
quy dinh ro moi su kien. Hien chua duoc xac nhan co thong bao rieng cho moi
lan chuyen cot va moi binh luan; chi bo sung neu tieu chi nghiem thu yeu cau.


4. Dashboard - DA CO CAC YEU CAU CHINH, CON MO RONG TUY CHON

Da co loc theo group, bieu do phan bo theo cot/tien do, thong ke dong gop
thanh vien va danh sach card qua han/sap den han. Chua co loc tuy y theo
thanh vien, trang thai hoac khoang thoi gian, va chua xuat bao cao. Cac muc
nay la mo rong, chi can lam neu giang vien dua vao tieu chi cham diem.


5. Tich hop Moodle Assignment - DA CO LIEN KET VA DASHBOARD CHI DOC

Form cho phep chon Assignment trong cung khoa hoc. Board hien ten/link/han nop;
dashboard doc trang thai nop va diem theo quyen mod_assign:viewgrades/grade.
Voi separate groups, dashboard yeu cau chon group; voi blind marking, du lieu
chi hien khi co quyen xem danh tinh an danh. Feedback duoc mo qua giao dien
Assignment goc de Moodle tu kiem tra quyen.

Tich hop khong ghi vao Assignment: khong tao submission/grade khi doc, khong
sua feedback va khong tu dong cap nhat card. Mapping card voi submission hoac
dong bo trang thai tu dong van can xac nhan neu giang vien yeu cau.


III. CAC PHAN CAN HOAN THIEN
----------------------------

6. Lich su thao tac day du

History hien ghi nhan cac thao tac card chinh va mot so su kien deadline.
Chua thay history cho them/xoa tep hoac thay doi cot/WIP limit. Can bo sung
nhung su kien nay neu bao cao cam ket nhat ky day du.

- Tao card.
- Sua tieu de va mo ta.
- Sua deadline.
- Sua assignee.
- Di chuyen card.
- Xoa card.
- Them va xoa file dinh kem.
- Them binh luan.
- Thay doi cot hoac WIP limit.

Moi ban ghi history hien co nguoi thao tac va thoi gian; noi dung truoc/sau
cua cac truong card chua duoc ghi nhan day du.


7. Phan quyen va bao mat

Plugin da kiem tra dang nhap, capability, context, sesskey tren upload,
quan he cmid/Kanban/card/cot va quyen group tren cac API. Da co PHPUnit kiem
tra API liet ke/xoa file theo quyen group. File pluginfile va truy cap
cross-Kanban, thay doi membership va tung vai tro van can kiem thu.

Da bo sung Moodle Privacy API cho nguoi tao, thanh vien/tu danh gia, assignee,
comment, history va tep dinh kem. Da them test provider cho metadata, context,
export va xoa rieng mot user ma khong lam mat du lieu chung/nguoi dung khac.
Da chay PHPUnit tren Moodle 4.3.12; test provider PASS.


8. Ngon ngu va giao dien

PHP co language pack tieng Viet/tieng Anh cho phan lon chuoi. Can ra soat
chuoi hard-code trong PHP/JavaScript, chay ESLint va kiem tra responsive,
modal va thong bao loi tren cac Moodle theme muc tieu.


9. Xu ly loi va du lieu bien

- Hien thi loi khi deadline khong hop le.
- Hien thi loi khi WIP limit da day: da them xu ly server-side va chuoi
  tieng Viet/tieng Anh cho tao card, upload card va chuyen cot.
- Hien thi loi khi file khong dung dinh dang hoac vuot qua dung luong.
- Hien thi loi khi assignee khong thuoc group hop le.
- Xu ly truong hop card hoac column da bi xoa trong luc nguoi dung dang thao tac.
- Khong de request loi tao ra du lieu khong day du.


IV. KIEM THU CAN BO SUNG
------------------------

10. PHPUnit

Da co test PHPUnit co ban cho group access, di chuyen card, WIP khi tao va
deadline; da them test WIP khi chuyen cot, thu tu card va API file. Suite
mod_kanban_testsuite da PASS tren Moodle 4.3.12/PHP 8.2.4/MariaDB 10.4.28
(20 tests, 100 assertions).
Van can bo sung:

- Tao/sua/xoa va sap xep column da co test.
- Sap xep card trong cung column.
- Them, sua va xoa assignee.
- PHPUnit da test helper luu nhieu tep, tra cuu noi dung file qua cung ham
  phan quyen cua pluginfile, chan group khac va API liet ke/xoa file. Van can
  kiem thu HTTP multipart/phan hoi tai file tren trinh duyet.
- Them va doc binh luan.
- Ghi nhan history.
- Kiem thu cron/thong bao thu cong; scheduled-task PHPUnit da PASS.
- Bao mat file theo group.
- Xoa activity va kiem tra du lieu lien quan duoc xoa.
- Backup/restore day du tat ca bang va file.


11. Behat va kiem thu giao dien

Behat feature mo ta mot so luong ben duoi. Can xac nhan cac step co duoc
cung cap boi moi truong Behat va chay feature thuc te; hien chua co ket qua
nghiem thu tu moi truong nay.

- Giao vien tao activity Kanban.
- Sinh vien trong group A khong xem duoc card cua group B.
- Tao card, sua card, xoa card va keo tha card.
- Phan cong mot hoac nhieu thanh vien.
- Them file dinh kem va mo chi tiet file.
- Them binh luan.
- Hien thi card sap den han va qua han.
- Hien thi dashboard.
- Quan ly column.
- Backup va restore activity.


12. Kiem thu tuong thich

README ghi nhan mot lan chay Moodle 4.3/MariaDB truoc cac thay doi hien tai.
Can chay lai PHPUnit va kiem tra cai dat/nang cap tren Moodle muc tieu; kiem
tra PostgreSQL, theme va Docker neu dua vao pham vi nghiem thu. De cuong chi
liet ke cong cu Docker, khong bat buoc phai dong goi Docker neu giang vien
khong yeu cau.


V. CAC NOI DUNG CAN CAP NHAT TRONG BAO CAO
-------------------------------------------

13. Lam ro pham vi san pham

Trong bao cao, tach ro:

- Chuc nang da hoan thanh.
- Chuc nang moi chi hien thi tren giao dien.
- Chuc nang chua co co che tu dong.
- Chuc nang du kien phat trien them.

Vi du:

- Card qua han va canh bao deadline: scheduled task co co che chong gui lap;
  neu chua co assignee/khong gui duoc thi khong danh dau da thong bao va se
  thu lai o lan chay sau. Test toggle/dedup/retry da PASS trong PHPUnit.
- Assignment: dashboard doc trang thai submission/diem theo quyen; khong ghi
  submission/grade/feedback va khong tu dong doi trang thai card.
- Dashboard: co group filter, bieu do, dong gop va deadline; chua co bo loc
  nang cao hoac xuat bao cao.
- WIP: duoc kiem tra o server khi tao/upload card va chuyen cot; test tao/chuyen
  da PASS, endpoint upload van can kiem thu.
- Privacy API va test provider: da bo sung va PHPUnit da PASS.


14. Cap nhat tieu chi nghiem thu

Tieu chi nghiem thu nen phan biet tinh nang da co va ket qua da kiem chung:

- Giao vien co the quan ly cac column.
- Thu tu card duoc luu dung sau khi keo tha.
- Thanh vien nhan dung notification; scheduled task deadline chay qua cron.
- Dashboard loc theo group (da co); loc thanh vien chi la yeu cau neu duoc giao.
- Khong lo du lieu giua cac group.
- File dinh kem chi duoc tai boi nguoi co quyen.
- Backup/restore phuc hoi dung card, column, file, comment va history.
- PHPUnit va Behat chay thanh cong (Behat: 4 scenario, 22 buoc PASS tren test site rieng).
- Plugin cai dat duoc tren moi truong Moodle muc tieu.


VI. THU TU UU TIEN DE THUC HIEN
-------------------------------

Uu tien 1 - Can lam truoc khi nghiem thu:

1. PHPUnit cho WIP tao/chuyen card, upload helper, Privacy, Assignment va
   deadline da PASS; van can kiem tra HTTP endpoint upload thu cong.
2. Behat 4 scenario/22 buoc da PASS tren test site rieng; duy tri chay lai sau
   khi sua quyen group, WIP hoac giao dien va tich hop CI neu can.
3. Kiem thu quyen file va notification/cron thu cong; backup/restore da co ket
   qua CLI ghi trong README.
4. Cap nhat bao cao theo dung pham vi Assignment va ket qua da chay.

Uu tien 2 - Nen lam de nang chat luong do an:

5. PHPUnit Privacy API da PASS; kiem tra export/delete tren giao dien Moodle.
6. Ra soat history/file audit, chuoi ngon ngu, responsive va theme.
7. Kiem thu PostgreSQL neu cam ket database nay trong pham vi.

Uu tien 3 - Tuy theo pham vi de tai:

8. Xac nhan voi giang vien co can mapping card va dong bo trang thai tu dong
   voi Assignment khong.
9. Them dashboard filter/export hoac CI/Docker neu pham vi do an yeu cau.


VII. KET LUAN
-------------

Plugin da dap ung phan lon chuc nang trong de cuong, bao gom quan ly cot/card,
phan quyen group, dashboard, notification va backup/restore. Viec con lai
truoc nghiem thu la chay lai test, kiem tra cac luong Moodle thuc te va
thong nhat voi giang vien ve muc tich hop Assignment. Privacy API da duoc bo
sung; can kiem thu PHPUnit trong moi truong Moodle co PHP.
